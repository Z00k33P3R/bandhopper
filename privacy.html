BandHopper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

BandHopper is a radio scanner app for Android that works with a USB software-defined radio (SDR) stick. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Information the app collects

None. BandHopper has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores your settings (such as squelch and gain) and the memory channels you save. These stay on your phone in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the app's developer never receives it.

Permissions

  • USB access to the radio stick you plug in. Android asks you before the app can use it.
  • Notifications to show what the scanner is doing while it runs in the background.
  • Foreground service and wake lock so scanning and audio continue with the screen off.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Radio signals

The app receives and plays radio signals live. It does not record or store what it receives.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
